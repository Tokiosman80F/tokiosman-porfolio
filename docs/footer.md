## SEO & accessibility notes

1. Wrap the footer in a `<footer>` element.
2. Add `aria-label` to icon-only social links (e.g. `aria-label="Toki Osman on GitHub"`) and `rel="me noopener"` with `target="_blank"`.
3. Use `mailto:tokiosman@outlook.com` for the email link and `https://wa.me/8801987556588` for WhatsApp.
4. Add Person structured data (JSON-LD) in the layout: name, job title "Full-Stack Developer", URL, email, and `sameAs` links to GitHub and LinkedIn. This helps Google connect your name to your profiles.
5. Generate the copyright year with `new Date().getFullYear()` so it never goes stale.
