# Competitor research: custom projects

Done 2026-11-02 for `/services/custom-projects`. Nearly all of these figures are what sellers publish about themselves, and I found no independent benchmark. Treat them as directional, not as market data. Upwork and Fiverr listings blocked direct reading (HTTP 403), so those are known from search result snippets only.

## Who the competition is

| Group | Examples | Published entry price | How you get a firm price | Weakness to answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marketplace freelancers | [Contra: discovery sprint](https://contra.com/s/ABZgMYsg-paid-discovery-sprint-working-mvp-in-days), [Contra: internal tools](https://contra.com/s/rePrB7mo-custom-internal-tools-and-operational-dashboards) | "Starting at $750" or "Contact for pricing" | A call | Most show no terms on code ownership, hosting or guarantees, and little proof |
| Small agencies | [ShooGa](https://shooga.ca/en/services/web-development/web-apps/), [Tendo Digital](https://brand-blitz-labs.lovable.app/services/nextjs-react-builds) | No figure, or an unexplained "From $500" with a 6–12 week timeline | After discovery (ShooGa); written scope and fixed quote within 48 hours (Tendo) | Vague pricing |
| Established software houses | [Magora](https://magora-systems.com/fixed-price-software-development-uk/), [Wavect](https://wavect.io/services/mvp-development/), [Bolder Apps](https://www.bolderapps.com/services/paid-discovery) | MVP £10k–£25k; typical cross-platform app €25k–€50k; "from $30K" | Paid discovery first: from £3,000 (2 weeks), €3,500 (2–3 weeks, deducted from the build) | Cost and time before a price, but strong proof (Clutch reviews, named clients) |
| AI app builders and rescue specialists | [Rohit Raj](https://rohitraj.tech/notes/lovable-alternative-developer-when-ai-builder-breaks), [Fora Soft](https://www.forasoft.com/blog/article/lovable-app-bugs-fix-cost) | Rescue $2,500–$8,000 over 2–4 weeks; audit only $750–$1,500 (Rohit Raj) | Audit first | A growing and specific demand, led by missing Supabase row-level security |

Rates for context: US mid-level freelance Next.js developer $89–$140/hr ([Second Talent](https://www.secondtalent.com/cost-to-hire/nextjs-developer/)); Bangladesh-based listings run from about $5 to $40/hr ([Hubstaff Talent, GoodFirms](https://www.goodfirms.co/companies/web-development-agency/nextjs/bangladesh)).

## What this changed on the page

1. **Starting prices are published.** Most custom offers hide them. Three size bands let visitors qualify themselves before booking. The bands follow the site's existing prices: $500 (Single integration), $2,000 (AI Upgrade), $4,500 (Web App MVP).
2. **Two routes to a fixed price.** [Hapy's red-flag list](https://hapy.co/journal/mvp-development-red-flags/) warns about confident estimates before discovery, which is a fair challenge to "a quote in 24 hours". So the promise stays for clear jobs, and unclear jobs get a short paid scoping step, priced well under agency discovery and deducted from the build.
3. **The written scope is the visible deliverable.** Buyers are told to ask "what is excluded from this number?" (Hapy), so the page lists what they get in writing, including what's left out.
4. **Ownership is stated plainly.** Marketplace listings mostly stay silent on it, while Wavect and Tendo state it outright.
5. **AI-built app rescue is named as a job.** Toki already lists Lovable and Supabase in the stack, and few solo portfolios target this directly.
6. **The limits of one person are stated.** Software houses win on team size and proof. The page says when an agency is the better choice instead of competing there.

## Where Toki is weaker

- **Proof.** Magora and Wavect show Clutch ratings, named clients and case studies. This site has no real testimonials or case studies yet (`PRODUCT.md`), so the page shows no proof block. This is the biggest gap against every competitor group.
- **Price anchoring.** Bangladesh-based listings start far lower, so the page competes on directness, fixed scope and written terms, not on being cheapest.
