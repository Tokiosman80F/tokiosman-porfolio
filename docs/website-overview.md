## 3. How a project runs

1. **Free 30-min call** — goals, scope, what to build.
2. **Fixed quote in 24 hours** — clear scope, fixed price and a launch date before any work starts.
3. **Design** — 3 homepage directions; the client picks one (websites).
4. **Build, in the open** — a preview link and a written progress update every working day.
5. **Revisions and launch** — revision rounds, SEO setup, deployment and domain. Hosting stays in the client's name.
6. **Support** — per plan (14–90 days), then maintenance plans or quoted work.

**How AI is used:** research, design exploration, first-pass code, tests and docs. Toki makes the calls and reviews everything that ships.
